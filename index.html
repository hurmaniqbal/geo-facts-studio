Geo Facts Studio

Short, fact-checked geography videos.

Geo Facts Studio is a personal tool that makes short geography videos on its owner's own computer and posts them to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else's account is used.

Every video is labelled as AI-generated, and every fact in it is checked against published sources before it is made.
