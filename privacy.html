Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

What the app accesses

When the owner connects their TikTok account, the app receives through TikTok's official API: basic profile information (display name, username, open ID), profile statistics (follower, following, like and video counts), the list of the account's public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts, and permission to post videos to that account.

How it is used

Only to post the owner's videos to their own account and to show the owner how those videos perform, so better videos can be made. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Where it is stored

Access tokens and statistics stay on the owner's own computer, in a folder readable only by the owner. Video statistics are processed by the AI assistant the owner uses to plan videos. No other server stores this data.

Other people's data

The app does not collect data about viewers or any other TikTok user.

Deleting data

Revoke access in TikTok (Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps), and delete the app's local folder. That removes all data the app holds.
